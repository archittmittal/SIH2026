# SIH 2026: Digital Twin for Well-to-Surface Optimization of CSS and SRP Operations

**Problem Statement ID:** SIH26120  
**Title:** Digital Twin for Well-to-Surface Optimization of Cyclic Steam Stimulation (CSS) and Sucker Rod Pump (SRP) Operations for Heavy Oil Wells of Baghewala Field  
**Organization:** Oil India Limited (OIL)  
**Category:** Software | Smart Automation  

## Overview
The Baghewala Field in Rajasthan produces heavy crude oil (17-19° API) from the Jodhpur Sandstone reservoir. Due to high viscosity, high asphaltene content, and low reservoir pressure/temperature (46-48°C), the oil has poor mobility. Currently, Cyclic Steam Stimulation (CSS) and Sucker Rod Pump (SRP) operations are optimized in silos, resulting in suboptimal oil recovery, high Steam-Oil Ratios (SOR), and frequent mechanical failures (e.g., rod floating, pump unsetting).

This project proposes **TwinFlow-AI**, a unified, AI-driven Digital Twin that integrates reservoir, wellbore, and surface SRP dynamics. TwinFlow-AI enables real-time monitoring, predictive maintenance, and closed-loop optimization to maximize cumulative production while minimizing operational costs and energy consumption.
